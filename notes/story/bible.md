# Moonlit Beacon — story setting

The title is the answer. This setting book is fitted so the game's existing
devices (four beacons, cycles, things that swarm, three Guardians plus three
promoted forms) all become the story's evidence.
**Do not make new screens or new art.** What is already on screen becomes the
setting as-is, and writing is laid one line at a time only in places that
already show text.

## One paragraph

As the forest fell, **the dark** spread. Wherever trees vanished and fire went
out, the dark pooled, and things came out of it. Things that lost their light
and drift, things that ate the dark and grew large — they look different, but
they do one thing. They gnaw away the places we live. That is how forest
became field, and field became ash.
There is only one way left. **Light the moonlit beacons and redraw our
ground.**

## The title's answer

**A moonlit beacon is not a fire that burns. It is a fire that draws a
territory.**

Ordinary fire only pushes the dark back for a moment, and when it dies the
dark returns as it was. A moonlit beacon holds the moon's light, so it does
not go out. From the place a beacon stands, the land outward becomes **ours**
again. Raise four and that night's territory is complete; fail and it presses
in from every side. So this game is not about killing every enemy. It is a
**game of raising territory**.

## Things that came out of the dark

They are not one kind. They come out differently depending on what the dark
swallowed.

- **Things that lost their light** — leftover shapes of what once lived on
  this land. They drift faintly like lanterns and gather at beacon fire. Not
  from hate, but because **they remember the light**.
  (the seven spirits in the game)
- **Things the dark raised** — beasts and insects grown strange in the dark.
  Large and hard, they try to swallow a beacon whole. (the Guardians in the
  game)

Both arrive in the same night. One is drawn to the light; the other wants to
erase it.

## Why this game's rules become the story

| Game device | Story |
| --- | --- |
| Light four beacons | Drive stakes in every direction and draw that night's territory. All four must stand for it to be complete |
| The moment before lighting is the most dangerous | Land without territory belongs to the dark |
| Cycles repeat | One night reclaims only a span of ground. The next night starts again from a wider place |
| Enemies grow stronger each cycle | This is land where the dark has pooled longer. The deeper it is, the larger the thing that comes out |
| Forest → field → camp | Not the order of reclaiming, but **the order of loss**. The field was once forest |
| Moon Dew drops on a kill | The light they had swallowed. Pick it up and put it in the beacon |
| The Guardian comes last | Something that grew by swallowing beacons. It ate light, so it shines, and that is why it is strong |
| Promoted Guardian | An individual that remembers the taste of an earlier night's beacon. It hunts the beacon from the start |
| Death leaves shards | Scattered moonlight is something even the dark cannot eat. The next night's beacon is that much brighter |
| Strengthen at the shrine | Using gathered moonlight to revive forgotten beacon craft |

## Characters

- **Moonlit Warden** (child) — the child left by the last beacon keeper.
  Lights beacons with the single beacon candle at the chest. Does not know
  where the seed came from.
- **Beacon Keeper** — someone who lost a village while guarding beacons. The
  lantern holds the first beacon's ember.
- **Moonlit Dancer** — a messenger who carried light between beacons with
  dance.
- **Silver Knight** — the side that tried to cut the dark with a sword.
  Learned that cutting does not shrink it, and follows the child.
- **Eclipse Mage** — someone who studied the dark until they came closest to
  it.
- **Sage** — the person who learned how to put moonlight into fire. Designer
  of the beacon system.

## Guardians — things that swallowed beacons

The names are already in the game. All three were things that lived on that
land, swallowed by the dark and grown.

- **Thornwood Pursuer** — swallowed the forest beacon. Charges straight.
- **Wings of the Blue Field** — swallowed the field beacon. Spreads a barrage
  with its wings.
- **Ember Ironclad** — swallowed the camp beacon. Its shell is scorched.

## Ending

On the last night, the child joins the beacons into one. A moon rises on the
ground. The dark recedes, the things inside it lose form and scatter, and the
ones that had lost their light find their shapes and grow quiet. What remains
is the reclaimed land, and the child grown faint from burning the seed.
When the forest returns, the moon in the sky is visible again — then there is
no need to light a beacon.

## Sentence rules

- One line at a time. Do not exceed banner/balloon length (about 24 Korean
  characters).
- Do not explain. Write only what the child **just noticed**.
- Do not put cycle numbers in sentences. The screen already says which loop
  this is.
- Only sentences that can move into five languages. Avoid puns.

## Cycle-banner draft (8 lines)

1. This is today's ground. Raise the beacons.
2. They were not hunting me. They only remember the firelight.
3. Four beacons, and it grows quiet. Leave one empty, and every side howls.
4. They said this field was once a forest.
5. The one that swallowed shines. The one that shines is large.
6. Cutting the dark does not shrink it. Push it back, and it shrinks.
7. The beacon was not a fire that burns. It was a fire that draws a territory.
8. Four is not enough. They must be joined into one.

## Guardian entrance lines

- Thornwood Pursuer — the one that swallowed the forest beacon.
- Wings of the Blue Field — it ate the field's light and grew wings.
- Ember Ironclad — soot remains on the shell.
- The three promoted forms — they remembered the taste of the beacon and came.

## Result-screen closers

- Cycles 1–2: Tonight my territory is as much as I kept.
- Cycles 3–4: Look back, and fire stands on every ridge.
- Cycles 5–6: The dark grew deeper. The night grew longer.
- Cycle 7 and after: This direction is right. Join them into one.

## Art — leave it as-is

The seven spirits (ghost forms) and the Guardians already in the game
**already match this setting.** Ghost forms are "things that lost their
light"; Guardians are "things the dark raised." Mixing both in one night is
natural in the setting. Do not redraw them.
