# feat(game): renew moonlit beacon for 4.0.0

Moonlit Beacon 4.0.0 keeps the original tap-to-start title and compact menus while adding Firebase guest, Google and Apple accounts. Each player has a durable public ID, account-partitioned progress, an explicit cloud-save conflict choice and a Hall showing character, score and competition rank. Resume restarts the saved gate segment. Starting a new journey asks before deleting that gate save; completed records remain.

All six heroes now move their painted bodies, hands and weapons during primary attacks: Warden follows through on a sword cut, Dancer alternates two separately held fangs, Eclipse turns its scythe through a broad arc, and Keeper, Knight and Sage have distinct recoil and recovery. Idle, walk, attack and recovery keep the same canonical face and head scale. All 24 idle facings have a neutral supported stance, idle breathing moves only the torso, and the title party shares one fit and ground anchor when walking and stopping. The physical motion preserves damage, cadence, projectile counts and sidearm ownership.

The world, guardian states and inner screens use painted artwork. Login buttons use official artwork, left-aligned icons and centered labels; guest entry sits last. Entry loading, delayed Hall responses and audio teardown have lifecycle coverage. Five-language privacy, Terms, support and the 16-lesson course/reference are managed in this monorepo and served by Firebase Hosting at https://moonlitbeacon.hyo.dev, with Vercel DNS and the Pages mirror retained.
