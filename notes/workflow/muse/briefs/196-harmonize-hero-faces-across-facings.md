# Brief 196: Harmonize hero face proportions across facings

## The ask
“근데 기본 캐릭터 윗모습이랑 아래모습 대비 좌우 얼굴이 너무 작은데? 대비가 심해”
“ㄷㅏ른것도 그런거 아냐..?”
The user noticed a different apparent face size when turning, after the previous repair stabilized each facing through walk, stop and attack. Audit all six heroes and correct the confirmed cross-facing proportion mismatch before the next iOS submission.

## Why, and what good feels like
Turning must read as the same premium painted character. A true profile naturally shows less skin width than a frontal face; that does not justify a noticeably smaller skull, compressed forehead-to-chin height, tiny eye or a different chibi proportion. Preserve the approved frontal identities. In particular Warden and Knight currently read smaller-faced and more adult/slender in profile. Never solve this by enlarging the entire side body, freezing the gait or giving the idle a different head than the walk.

## Where things stand
Main contains merged PR 11. All six heroes currently share an exact canonical head per facing across idle, walk and attack torso, with neutral supported feet. The missing contract is consistency BETWEEN facings. Current production is generated by apps/game/tools/pack_painted_world.py from notes/workflow/muse/art/4-0-0 turnarounds and gait donors; pack_attack_rig.py derives the attack torso and arm patches from idle. The side-walk donor and the turnaround side head are distinct. The director viewed all 24 current native-facing cells and all six current head boards, plus the fitted turnaround-side alternatives for Warden, Knight and Eclipse.

The director measured alpha >=64 head-band bounds on current idle row 0, using HERO_IDLE_NECK:
- Warden down/up 59/57 pixels wide; side 47. All bands 46px high.
- Keeper 68/67, side 60; 44px high.
- Knight 45/42, side 41; 36px high.
- Sage 37/37, side 54; 34/33px high (the side ponytail inflates width).
- Dancer 72/78, side 72; 46px high (pigtails inflate width).
- Eclipse 58/57, side 55; 42px high.
These bands include clothing/hair and end at the fixed neck, so they are NOT sufficient face-size measurements. Equal band heights already passed while the user-visible defect remained. Inspect and measure actual skull/face landmarks, excluding hair tails, hood decorations and clothing. Warden's fitted turnaround-side head is 63px wide versus current gait head 47 at the same total body fit; it is promising comparison material, not an approved unregistered replacement.

Existing contracts: tests/test_hero_visuals.gd, tests/test_hero_gait.gd, tests/test_hero_attack_motion.gd, tools/test_painted_world_checks.py; detailed previous work in notes/workflow/muse/hero-attack-replacement-validation.md. The gait test pins idle/portrait/front bytes. Re-pin only intentional reviewed art changes, retain every behavior check.
