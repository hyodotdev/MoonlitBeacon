# Brief 094: keep the live voice strip inside the viewport

## The ask and confirmed evidence
The user wants polished creative screens and a live tutorial without perceived freezes. In the actual ordinary Android 4.0.0 arena the voice strip at the bottom was visibly clipped. The director reproduced independently with real `VoicePanel.say` and the current Warden resource, then measured after five frames: the intended root height is 34, but Box minimum/height is 78, Row/Portrait is 72, and Box/Row/Portrait/Text lie outside the viewport. The painted hero head crop is 72×72; `_icon` has custom minimum 24 but its default TextureRect expand mode retains its native minimum. Existing source sets only stretch mode. This is a confirmed intrinsic-size regression after the painted portraits replaced older art.
