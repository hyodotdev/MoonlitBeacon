# Brief 065: the actual 4.0.0 entry and persistent player journey

## The ask
“구글 게임 로그인 애플로그인 게스트 로그인 넣고 마지막 플레이한 곳에서 플레이하고 명예의 전당 붙여서 계속 랭크 보여주면 어때? ... 4.0.0 ... 플레이어 아이디는 고유로 ... 게스트 로그인도 미리 아이디 받고 ... 이런느낌으로 로딩도 깔끔하게 보완 ... firebase도 로그인했어 잘 부탁해”. Build the production playable flow from the independently accepted foundations, rather than another standalone demo.

## Why, and what good feels like
First the animated moon gate appears cleanly. A player sees their durable ID before entering, chooses a genuine configured provider or a working local guest, and continues at their saved gate with their character and growth. A short guide accompanies live movement and combat. The Hall shows actual characters, scores and full unique IDs; the current rank remains readable during the journey. Conflicts and offline states are honest and never replace a save silently. Reference layout can inspire the flow; original painted Moonlit art and existing music define its own world.

Root contains accepted Journey/Onboarding/StoryEpisodes, CloudCoordinator and its owned data/HTTP services, Native PlayerAccount/IdentityAdapter/bridge, GateEntry/GateLoadingOverlay/panels, and painted actor/world/weapon components as present in this copy. Read `notes/setup/cloud-coordinator-contract.md` exactly: its durable baseline, account partition migration and foreign-integer receipt mapping are critical. The director has reproduced local rewards as 0 → 15 → 15 with failures/retries, foreign cloud progress preserving conflict, newer seals surviving commits, and generation-owned upload locking. Do not bypass these boundaries.

Current production still boots the old title, which has working Shrine, Shop/IAP, Settings, Credits, Chronicle, music and capture proof hooks. It already has Journey start/resume/fresh-confirmation behavior. GateEntry is accepted PRESENTATION, with real host signals but no services. GateLoadingOverlay paints two frames, uses ResourceLoader's real progress, guards generation and drains cancelled loads. Its host must wait for `has_pending_drain()` before freeing it. Both native providers on both OSes are being completed separately; use capability data honestly, never success fixtures in production. `NativeIdentityAdapter.refresh_native_session()` is the new SDK-session hydration contract; cached `get_session()` must stay a snapshot.
