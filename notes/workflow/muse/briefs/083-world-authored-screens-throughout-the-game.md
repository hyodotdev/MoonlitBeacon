## Creative direction / actual deliverable
Make each screen feel physically and narratively part of the moon gate journey, with a coherent deep-ink/aged metal/moon-blue/ember-gold language and original local editable UI art. Not a uniform recolor of old rectangular dialogs. Use authored texture/vector frames and layered, role-specific compositions: strong silhouettes, real supplied art, intentional negative space, depth/shadows and clear action hierarchy. No default Godot button face, plain generic rounded app card, gray prototype, new internet asset pack or text baked into paintings. Existing fonts/CJK fallback remain.

- Shrine / hero selection / hero preview: a guardian exhibition. Actual hero, recognisable weapon and full-body presentation as the centre, distinct hero accent, readable role/ownership and deliberate selection rail. Current currency/boon/unlock/IAP behavior and product IDs remain exact. Showing a hero is never granting ownership.
- Chronicle / chapter act card / dialogue: an illuminated journey book / chapter threshold, with real episodes, discovered/undiscovered distinction, place/character motifs and purposeful page progression. Keep all genuine story IDs, choices, timings and live tutorial; never introduce a modal tutorial pause again.
- Shop / IAP: a curated relic-and-guardian display, with actual product/hero/relic artwork, differentiated currency vs money and readable real price/status/restore/cancellation/retry. NO extra purchase tap/auto-purchase, no new paywall, simulated price or fake ownership. Source logos/bundle portraits are not generic placeholders.
- Results / ladder: a journey record with hero, score and recovered embers as a clear visual achievement, distinct defeat versus gate-cleared moods, thoughtful stamps/medals and primary retry/continue route. Keep payout and ledgers, account-scoped run continuation and truthfully sourced rank. Real empty ladder stays empty.
- HUD / pause / dash / choices / relic picks: compact crafted combat instruments, recognisable beacon/weapon/relic symbols and emphatic dash/choice affordances. Do not cover combat, shrink text to fit, change button hitboxes arbitrarily, interrupt controls, change mechanics or add a busy effect layer. Clarify the beacon's two real choices visually with their exact actual effects. Preserve all HUD safe-area, charge, missile/core, damage, progress and tutorial information.
- Settings / credits / quit / analytics consent: the same crafted world UI, each with its appropriate identity (field journal / makers' colophon / gate-return confirmation). Clear real language/sound/accessibility and opt-in choices remain. No invented terms/legal/permissions, consent changes or dark patterns.
- The old title compatibility scene should use the coherent controls/fonts and still pass its suites, but do not redesign its old diorama or steal ownership of the new Gate intro task.
