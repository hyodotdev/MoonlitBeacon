# Brief 043: Dimensional painted art across the playable world

## The ask
The user said “그리고 지금 여전히 겜이 약간 픽셀 아트 같은데 스프라이트로 하더라도 우린 좀 고급 이미지 느낌을 살리고 샆어 약간 입체감 있는 느낌 악튜러스 같은”, supplied title/login/loading references, then asked us to reflect all of it in 4.0.0. This round replaces the playable world's pixel-art appearance with original shaded 2.5D artwork. Title/login/loading UI integration follows separately.

## Where things stand
Original generated source atlases are in `notes/workflow/muse/art/4-0-0/`: six `<hero>-turnaround.png` files (Warden is 1234×1275; the others 1254×1254), `biome-props.png` (4 columns×6 biome rows), `biome-floors.png` (3 columns×2 rows), `spirit-turnarounds.png` (4 columns×7 species rows), `guardian-states.png` (4 columns×6 species rows), and `moon-gate-title.png`. The source masters were produced with the built-in image generator and are original; the user's commercial reference screenshots must not be copied into the game. Existing sprites are tiny 48×64 hero cells and similarly small creature/terrain cells. Global filtering and the 808×360 logical canvas are locked; `canvas_items` renders at device resolution, so high-resolution sources and per-item filtering can improve image quality without changing gameplay coordinates.

## Do
- Deterministically pack the source atlases into practical high-resolution runtime art with measured alpha bounds, consistent feet and cell geometry. Keep four correct directions for every hero and ordinary enemy. Do not pretend mirrored front art is a back view. Preserve the six distinct weapon rigs and combat rules. Use source walking poses and light procedural idle/breathing movement, without distorting collision positions.
- Replace hero sheets and portraits/previews, ordinary spirits and guardian body/state art, and all six terrain floor/decor/obstacle sources with the painted set. Preserve species-specific mechanics; variant guardians must remain visually distinct through restrained materials/accents and proper attack cues. Atlas row mappings: spirits drifter/ember/caster/weaver/stalker/swarm/wisp; guardians forest/field/camp/frost/marsh/ruins; props forest/field/camp/frost/marsh/ruins; floor panels in the same order, across the top row then bottom row.
- Extend resource art metadata where needed (cell size, visual scale, filter, preview region) so world-space body, weapon muzzle and collision shapes remain correct. Keep global nearest filtering for legacy course content, but render the new painted images with appropriate smooth filtering. Remove extreme pixel-grid/pixel-border treatments from these assets without blurring text or combat cues.
- Preserve depth through grounded shadows, consistent sprite feet, y sorting and existing light/mist layers. Ensure the player remains visible near tall props and projectiles/telegraphs remain readable on quiet floors. Do not raise decor/enemy node counts; stay inside late-game performance limits. Old shader pixel-offset assumptions must be adjusted for new texture sizes.
- Add a complete asset/contact-sheet review harness and behavioral/resource checks for six heroes×four directions, seven ordinary species×four directions, all guardian variants/states and all six terrains. Register new checks without modifying the existing game regression runner in this round (another round edits it). Update asset manifest/contracts truthfully and include a reproducible packing/check command and source mapping record in an author-only build note.

## Do not
Do not touch title/result UI, arena progression, journey/account modules, localization, project/export values, package.json, backend, store images, purchases, guards or `notes/workflow/muse/`. Other rounds are modifying these independently. Do not generate replacement art with code, add commercial screenshots, download packs, or claim a concept sheet is gameplay evidence. No network, git, devices, store uploads or marketing recapture. Existing movement/attack/boss timing must stay intact.

## Acceptance
All specified production resources consume the new painted sources; old pixel art is not silently left in one terrain or paid hero. A review harness renders the actual production resources at the game camera and at source resolution in all listed directions/states. No cropped limbs, matte boxes, wrong-facing backs, floating feet, broken muzzle positions, alpha fringes, excessive glow, visually indistinguishable guardians or repeated obvious floor seams. New art passes deterministic packing/asset checks, script compilation and relevant hero/terrain/guardian/capture tests without weakening their production coverage. Late-game node/draw budgets do not regress. Provide actual image dimensions, runtime memory/asset-byte totals and commands; the director repeats them.

## Deliverables and judgment
Packed `apps/game/assets/custom/` art, source pack/check tool under `apps/game/tools/`, actor/resource/room visual integration, new direct-callable tests and a foreground `shot_painted_world` harness, asset-manifest/contract updates, and `notes/plans/4-0-0-art-build-log.md`. The director inspects every production sheet and representative moving gameplay, runs the related suites/performance checks and requests corrections on confirmed defects. Credit the original source generation separately from integration; no claim of physical device testing from desktop captures.
