# 3.0.0 — A deeper night, a longer road

## Goal

Give the shipped 2.1.0 game a new face, a shaped story and a longer road,
without moving what it already proved. Cycles 1 to 8 keep their shipped
numbers (tests pin them); everything new either starts past the win or is
additive. A 3.0.0 run has this rhythm:

`explore → kindle and choose → defend or move → fork a gate → Guardian → return or go deeper`

## Scope

This is PR preparation, not a store submission. Merge readiness is decided by
repo checks only. Store screenshot recapture, uploads, Firestore deployment,
signed distribution builds and store review are later operations: not done,
and not required to merge this code. 2.1.0 is the last recorded live version,
not newly audited in this round (no network audit).
3.0.0 is not live, submitted, tagged or merged.

The Lantern Hollow road-home renewal is accepted and in the tree: Nari's
missing signal, the kettle, six place memories restored with beacons, the
Wave-8 homecoming, and the voluntary expedition past the map. Store copy in
this run describes that game. Snapshot 2026-09-30 per director continuity:
gate-caption correction 010b is locally accepted and committed (71 gate /
24 quit / 248 staging cases); the director independently verified 71 / 248 /
951 / 50 with the actual clearance mutation failing nine assertions. It does
not change the accepted narrative. Build-log reconciliation via brief 015 and
the full final verify/build remain pending.

## What changed

Player-facing, in six lines (same list as the prep record in
[checklist.md](../release/checklist.md)): a new UI kit on every screen with a
combat-readable HUD, a result Depth caption with Wave wording, and whole shop
blurbs; Lantern Hollow road home — Nari's signal and kettle, three acts plus
an epilogue over cycles 1 / 3 / 6 / 9, six place memories restored with
beacons, a discovery guard, a pause objective, three endings with a settlement
window, a 40-entry chronicle, and per-hero voices; a re-lit night with all
mobs, guardians and heroes redrawn; an expedition to six places through forked
gates with three new guardians and mutations; an endless stretch past cycle 8
with a Depth curve, omens, Moonless Trials and nine skills; guardian attacks
drawn from volley data as soft coral/mint markers, plus slow readable bullets
from guardians and spirits to weave through.

How it is built (details in [3-0-0-build-log.md](./3-0-0-build-log.md),
[3-0-0-expedition.md](./3-0-0-expedition.md) and
[game.md](../../apps/docs/docs/game.md)):

- One UI kit: 36 nine-patch PNGs plus shared `resources/ui/**/*.tres` styles,
  generated by `tools/build_ui_kit.py` from one registry; all 17 UI scenes
  migrated, and the old panel/button art and its generator deleted. Real
  layout changes (not just a reskin) on the relic picker, combat HUD, pause,
  settings, result, run-choice modals and title. Result keeps its pinned
  geometry: the epitaph shares its line with Depth past cycle 8, the score
  row reads Waves in English, non-coin shop cards show three blurb lines,
  and the card gives Detail 120 with Road 16 below it.
- Road home with a shape: Lantern Hollow, Nari's signal and kettle; acts
  over cycles 1 / 3 / 6 / 9 with title cards; six place memories (one motif
  beside each beacon clearing, dim until lit, one discovery line on first
  restoration per run, one chronicle entry each, fork gates hinting the
  waiting memory); a discovery guard owning the voice strip for three
  seconds while same-beacon fork, guardian-meet and moonfire lines wait;
  a pause objective (road + Wave/Depth + places count); three endings where
  only the cycle-8 win brings Nari home (early return keeps the lamp
  waiting, defeat names the lamp); a 40-entry chronicle in its own file
  (`user://chronicle.json`, deliberately not in the Vault) with unmet
  entries as blanks; per-hero openings and moment lines for the five
  non-Warden heroes; the Moonless named at cycle 10. Five languages
  throughout (`moonlit.csv`, no ASCII comma in a cell).
- Night re-lit as one scene, moon upper left: shadows, rim and sway
  (`RoomShadows`, `world_polish.gdshader`), floor tone and flora, lantern
  pools (`RoomTone`, `RoomFlora`, `RoomLights`), mist, shafts and motes
  (`RoomAtmosphere`), a full-screen grade (`night_grade.gdshader`), one
  actor shader for heroes, spirits and guardians, and pooled kill bursts.
  Code only — no sheet was redrawn in that pass. Afterwards the seven mobs,
  six guardians and six heroes were redrawn from generator lineups
  (`cut_lineup.py`); heroes stay 36px tall in the same 48x64 cell, so no
  combat or layout code moved.
- Expedition rules in `Expedition` as pure functions of (run seed, cycle,
  zone): the six-place table, the fork draw, the difficulty curve, guardian
  mutations, omens and the Moonless Trial. Forks open only in real runs
  from the title, from cycle 2; a hand-built arena keeps the one classic
  gate.
- Every guardian volley is one angle list (`Spirit.volley_shape()`) feeding
  the bolts, the pictures and the bot's eyes. Fairness is held by
  `test_volley_fairness` (thirteen guardians, eighteen cycles up to 100)
  and `test_telegraph_floors` (minimum warning times).
- Readable bullets between volleys: one `bullet_field` of up to 150 small
  enemy orbs in packed arrays, shot by `bullet_emitter` spirals, aimed
  shots, rings and waves; guardians shed a thin aura plus a heavier stream
  while not winding up, casters, weavers and wisps throw fans, singles and
  rings, and chasers spit a rare slow single. Every orb is slower than the
  slowest hero, leaves wide gaps, fades at range end, stops on structures,
  dissolves when its guardian falls, and stays quiet for the first seconds
  in a new place. Held by `test_bullet_field` and `test_barrages`.

## Budgets and how they were measured

Every number below is a historical measurement quoted from the build log and
the expedition plan — comparison material, not a current pass.

- Node budget: the late-game arena reads 1176 of 1200 standing nodes (budget
  test; the render pass added about a dozen standing nodes). Room nodes by
  `count_terrain_nodes`: forest 766, frost 586, marsh 696, ruins 529;
  forest Lv20 1097, Lv40 1167.
- Bolt and mark caps: hostile bolts 24 in the air, ground marks 8 alive,
  skills drawn in one node. A fan is at most 69 degrees, a ring or cross
  keeps at least 16 degrees of clear way, a volley carries at most 22
  bolts, and windup floors are 0.45 s (0.75 s first fan, 0.85 s floor
  circle).
- Soak (`tools/soak_run.tscn`: seed 7, stand-still invulnerable bot, 60 s a
  zone, speed 4, 14 cycles): spirit lifetime 25 s to 28 s across the win
  (old curve 27 s to 36 s); kills a minute 33 then 19 (old 26 then 11);
  level 75 at cycle 14 (old 60); toughness 59 (old 279). No errors; all six
  places travelled, eight guardians met.
- Play bot (`tools/play_bot.tscn`, some 26 hours of play): isolated
  guardian gauntlet, 168 fights — 89% won, 1.4 hits a fight (cycles 2 to
  6: 72 of 72, 0.5 hits; cycles 8 to 14: 80%, 2.05 hits). Mortal natural
  loops: 7 of 12 alive on this tree vs 9 of 12 on 2.1.0, at 0.66 vs 0.64
  hits a minute — the same within twelve runs. Pacing: a zone 60 s to
  100 s, a guardian 35 s to 45 s, a loop about four and a half minutes.
- Render cost: the depth pass about 0.3 ms a frame on the dev Mac
  (windowed A/B per layer, vsync off).
- HUD pinned width 311px worst case (`tools/measure_hud.tscn`).
- Route evenness (`tools/route_stats.gd`, 400 seeds, cycles 3 to 14):
  every place opens 15 to 19% of the cycles and is offered at 15 to 18%
  of the gates.

Director diagnostics on the same development machine (reported for this
reconciliation; they supersede any "being repeated" status above, and older
batches stay as dated history). Automated numbers only; they do not establish
a human difficulty or fun verdict.

- One-fight gauntlet (`director_gaunt_one runs=18 seed=11 speed=3 loops=1
  gauntlet=1 guardians=0,1,2,3,4,5 starts=2,6,12`): 18 fights, 16 won
  (88.9%), mean 1.17 hits/fight, bullet peak 62, zero stuck/soft lock.
  Places and cycles were paired cyclically, not a full Cartesian matrix.
- Gauntlet-mode settle check (`director_mode runs=4 seed=11 speed=3 loops=2
  gauntlet=1 guardians=3 starts=2` on the corrected mode): all four runs
  settled after one fight, 41.6 seconds wall time.
- Natural diagnostic (`director_story_nat runs=12 seed=11 speed=3 loops=2`):
  61.05 simulated minutes, 0.753 hits/min, 46.47 scattered/min, 26.08
  percent mean weaving, bullet peak 73, six guardian fights, zero
  stuck/soft lock. A different seed/loop diagnostic, not the frozen seed-5
  calibration. The arena's final deferred-strip cleanup changed after this
  bot launched; combat did not change. Do not read it as a run of the exact
  final source.

## Known limitations

Quoted from the build log's and the expedition plan's "Not done" lists, with
corrections marked. The first-fork hint, result Depth caption with Wave
wording, shop blurbs, and the Lantern Hollow story are done, not limitations.

1. Store screenshots (phone, 7-inch, 10-inch, iPad) were not recaptured or
   uploaded. Marketing proofs are stale after the redrawn art, place motifs
   and UI changes; that staleness is reported here and does not authorize
   a recapture.
2. Corrected: the result screen carries Depth past the win on the epitaph
   line, the score row reads Waves in English, shop cards show whole blurbs,
   and the road line plus settlement window state the real road. A rank seal
   would still be a good next step.
3. Corrected: the six hero IAP images were regenerated and
   `check:store-graphics` passes. The marketing screenshots still show the
   2.1.0 art and are redone together with the store set, on the user's
   word.
4. The title screen keeps its own atmosphere and does not get the grade.
5. No player's-eye judgement of the curve: the soak shows the growth stays
   in step with a growing player and the bot shows the fights can be
   answered by something that reads the picture; only a person can say
   whether it is enjoyable or how hard it feels. Telemetry (depth
   reached, omen chosen) would help tuning.
6. An Echo on a ring guardian (field, forest, toad, sentinel) still trims
   the repeat under the 24-bolt cap while its picture shows the whole
   ring — the safe way to be wrong. Fixing it means fewer spokes with an
   Echo present or a higher cap, and the node budget has no room for the
   second.
7. Corrected: the first-fork hint exists — the arena says the fork line
   when a fork opens, with `VOICE_FORK_1/2` in all five languages.
8. The bot cannot say whether the stretch is fun, how a thumb on glass
   fares against a 0.45 s warning, or how the game feels at 120 fps on a
   phone.

## Steps to a store submission

Code merge requirements (1–2, repo checks only) are separate from later
release steps (3–8), which stay uncompleted until performed under separate
user authorization. Each step names its owner. `[director]` steps run on the
real tree; `[user]` steps need the user's hands, credentials or word.

1. [director] Re-confirm `pnpm check:hygiene` green on the real tree
   (green in this copy's snapshot).
2. [director] Full regression green on the final tree.
3. [director] Confirm on device, Android first then iPad; the iPad capture
   needs a sudo RSD tunnel started by the user [user]. No new
   device/capture authorization in this run.
4. [user, then director] Recapture store screenshots after the game freeze
   — only on the user's explicit word, minimum set only.
5. [user] Deploy `firestore.rules`, indexes and TTL; set privacy labels
   and the release `firebase.cfg`. Analytics collection remains gated.
6. [user] Store console steps: listings, uploads, review submit on both
   stores.
7. [director] Cut signed distribution builds on the real tree
   (credentials live outside the repo).
8. [user] Tag the release and attach the final products — a separate
   user-authorized release operation, never automatic after submission.
